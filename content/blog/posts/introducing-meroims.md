Most of what we do at AK Research Lab is infrastructure and engineering work for other teams. MeroIMS is different: it's a product we design, build, and run ourselves. This post explains what it is and why it exists.

## The problem

Small and growing businesses usually keep their records in several places at once. Stock is tracked in one tool or a notebook, invoices in another, and the accounts are reconciled at the end of the month, often by re-typing the same numbers. Every hand-off is a chance for something to drift out of sync, and VAT time makes the gaps obvious.

We wanted a single system where recording a sale is enough: stock goes down, the invoice is issued, and the books are updated, all from the same entry.

## What MeroIMS covers

MeroIMS connects the core parts of running a business:

- **Inventory**: real-time stock levels, batches and barcodes, automatic reorder, and transfers between locations.
- **VAT billing and e-invoicing**: VAT-ready invoices and returns prepared from your transactions, with IRD-registered billing available as an add-on.
- **Sales and point of sale**: fast checkout, quotations, and orders that stay in sync with stock.
- **Purchases**: purchase orders through to payment, supplier ledgers, and landed costs.
- **Double-entry accounting**: journals, ledgers, and financial statements generated from day-to-day activity, plus bank reconciliation.
- **Reports**: live dashboards, profit tracking, and a library of standard and custom reports.

Around that core sit modules for customers and CRM, projects and tasks, payroll, approvals, role management, and an audit trail.

## Where AI fits in

The **Mero AI Assistant** lets you ask questions about your business in plain language, by chat or by voice. It can generate reports on request, forecast which products are likely to run low, suggest purchases, and help with invoices, accounting questions, and contracts. The answers come from your own MeroIMS data rather than generic advice.

This is where our research work feeds directly into a product. Building an assistant that's useful for accounting means being careful about what it is allowed to do and making sure it works from the records the business actually keeps.

## Built for how businesses in Nepal work

MeroIMS was built in Nepal, for Nepali businesses. That shows up in the details: VAT billing, PAN and VAT information on customer and supplier records, and support for IRD-registered billing.
